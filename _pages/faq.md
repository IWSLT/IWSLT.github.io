### How do I participate in IWSLT?

You can participate in several ways:
- **Submit a scientific paper** through our submission portal or commitment with ARR reviews.
- **Take part in a shared task**, join one of the Shared Tasks and submit a system description paper describing your approach.
  Shared tasks are announced in January and submission takes place in March/April.
- **Organize a shared task** by submitting a shared task proposal to the IWSLT program committee (deadline is typically in September).
- **Attend the conference**, held yearly alongside ACL.

### How do I stay updated on IWSLT news and deadlines?

Check the [News](/news/) page on this website, join the [IWSLT Evaluation Campaign google group](https://groups.google.com/g/iwslt-evaluation-campaign){:target="_blank"}, and follow IWSLT on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/136094964){:target="_blank"}.

### How can I join a shared task?

Shared tasks are listed on the website and announced every year (in January).
Each task page contains its own instructions for participation.

### How do I become a member of SIGSLT?

SIGSLT membership is open to anyone interested in spoken language translation. Subscribe to the [SIGSLT google group](https://groups.google.com/g/sigslt){:target="_blank"} to join — see the [SIGSLT](/sigslt/) page for more information.

### Where can I find papers and results from previous editions?

Visit the [Past Editions](/past-editions/) page for links to previous conferences, programs, and shared task results.

### Who do I contact if I have a question that isn't answered here?

Reach out through the [IWSLT Evaluation Campaign google group](https://groups.google.com/g/iwslt-evaluation-campaign){:target="_blank"}, and a member of the organizing committee will get back to you.
